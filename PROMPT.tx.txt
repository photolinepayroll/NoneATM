Create a mobile-friendly GCash Payroll Enrollment Form for:

Photoline Enterprises Corporation

Title:
GCash Payroll Enrollment Form

Purpose:
For employees without an ATM Savings Account. Employees may enroll their own Verified GCash Account for salary crediting.

Requirements:
- Form responses must automatically save to Google Sheets.
- Create a dedicated Google Sheet file in Google Drive named:
  "Photoline GCash Payroll Enrollment Responses"
- Uploaded GCash screenshots and employee digital signatures must be stored securely in a Google Drive folder named:
  "GCash Payroll Enrollment Attachments"

Form Fields:

Section 1 – Employee Information

1. Employee Name (Required)
2. Branch/Department (Required)
3. Contact Number (Required)
4. Verified GCash Mobile Number (Required)

Section 2 – GCash Verification

5. Upload Screenshot of Verified GCash Account (Required)

Requirements:
- Screenshot must show:
  • Employee Full Name
  • Verified Status
  • GCash Mobile Number

Allowed File Types:
JPG, PNG, PDF

Maximum File Size:
10 MB

Section 3 – Employee Declaration

Required Checkbox:

"I certify that the GCash account provided is my own Verified GCash Account. I authorize Photoline Enterprises Corporation to credit my salary and approved payments to this account until I submit a written request for changes. I understand that any delay or failed transaction caused by incorrect information I provided will be my responsibility."

Section 4 – Electronic Signature

Create a required handwritten digital signature field.

Requirements:
- Employee signs using finger on mobile phone touchscreen.
- Signature must be saved as an image file.
- Store signature image in the Google Drive folder:
  "GCash Payroll Enrollment Attachments"
- Link the signature file to the Google Sheet response record.
- Include clear and re-sign option.

Section 5 – Submission Date

Automatically record submission date and time.

Confirmation Message:

"Thank you! Your GCash Payroll Enrollment Form has been successfully submitted. The HR Department of Photoline Enterprises Corporation will review your information and may contact you for additional verification."
